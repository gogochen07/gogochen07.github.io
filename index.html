I am currently a 3rd year ECE PhD student at Duke University focusing on AI + healthcare. I am fortunate to be advised by Prof. Maciej A. Mazurowski.

My research interests primarily lie in foundation models, generative models, learning under data distribution shifts and advancing AI solutions for real-world challenges. More details are in projects pages

Prior to my Ph.D., I earned master’s degrees in Electrical and Computer Engineering from Carnegie Mellon University, where I was advised by Prof. Zachary Lipton, and from Imperial College London, where I was supervised by Prof. Jesus Rodriguez Manzano. I completed my undergraduate studies through the 2+2 program at the University of Nottingham, where I primarily worked with Dr. David Siu Yeung Cho.
